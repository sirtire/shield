# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Shield** ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects
data when you or your server use the Bot on Discord. By inviting or using the Bot, you agree to
the practices described in this policy.

## 1. What the Bot Does

Shield is a security and moderation bot for Discord servers. It helps server administrators
protect their communities against raids, malicious admin actions, unwanted bots, and spam by
detecting those events and applying the countermeasures the server's administrators have
configured.

## 2. Data We Collect

Shield only collects the data it needs to provide its protection features:

- **Server information** — server ID, name, and the moderation and protection settings chosen
  by the server's administrators.
- **User identification data** — user ID and username of members involved in moderation events
  (for example, a user who was banned by the Bot or who triggered a protection).
- **Blacklists and whitelists** — user IDs entered by server administrators, together with the
  reason they provide for the entry.
- **Role membership records** — the list of member IDs that held certain roles designated by
  administrators for protection and restoration after an attack.
